What We Provide

From routine oil changes to full engine overhauls — Lanka Auto Parts delivers professional automotive services with genuine parts and expert technicians.

Routine Maintenance & Servicing

Oil and filter changes, air filter replacement, fluid top-ups (brake, coolant, steering), and spark plug replacement. Keep your vehicle running at peak performance with regular scheduled maintenance.

Brake System Services

Complete brake inspection, brake pad and rotor replacement, parking brake adjustment, and brake fluid flush. Ensure your safety with our thorough brake system diagnostics and repairs.

Tire & Wheel Services

Tire rotation, balancing, wheel alignment, patching, and full tire replacement. We ensure optimal traction, handling, and even wear for a smooth and safe ride.

Engine & Transmission Repair

Advanced diagnostic services, complete engine overhauls, and transmission repairs. From minor tuning to major rebuilds, our expert technicians restore your vehicle's power and efficiency.

Electrical & AC System Repairs

Battery checks and replacement, starter and alternator repair, wiring diagnostics, and A/C system recharging and servicing. Stay cool and connected on every journey.

Suspension & Steering

Inspection and repair of struts, shocks, ball joints, tie rods, and steering components. Restore ride comfort and vehicle stability with our suspension expertise.

Bodywork & Specialized Services

Accident repairs, professional painting, dent removal, undercarriage degreasing, and full car washing services. We restore your vehicle's appearance to showroom condition.

Diagnostic Services

Using advanced computerized tools to identify engine, transmission, and electrical issues accurately. Fast, precise diagnostics help us fix the root cause — saving you time and money.

About Lanka Auto Parts & Repairs

Over a decade of excellence in genuine spare parts, vehicle sales, and rental services across Sri Lanka.

A Legacy of Trust & Quality

Lanka Auto Parts & Repairs is a leading provider of genuine spare parts, vehicle sales, and rental services headquartered in Wattala, Sri Lanka. With over 10 years of experience in the automotive industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted name among vehicle owners and businesses alike.

Our deep expertise spans Japanese, Malaysian, and international vehicle brands including Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Mitsubishi, Suzuki, Mazda, Daihatsu, Perodua, and many more. We understand the unique requirements of each brand and model, ensuring you get the exact parts you need.

We are committed to delivering only authentic, high-quality components through our network of specialist partners, combined with exceptional customer service that puts your needs first.
